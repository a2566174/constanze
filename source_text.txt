日常英語文應用

English for Everyday Use

 

拓展英語技能，聚焦聽、說、讀、寫各方面的全面課程。透過沉浸式活動、互動式課程和定制練習，深化學員的語言能力。通過文本選讀與解析，加強英文語感，並更深入理解單字及片語背後的思考邏輯及文化脈絡。歡迎加入，開啟語言掌握之旅！

Expand English proficiency with a comprehensive course focusing on listening, speaking, reading, and writing. Through immersive activities, interactive lessons, and customized exercises, participants deepen their language skills. Enhance English language intuition through text analysis, gaining a deeper
understanding of the logic and cultural context behind vocabulary and phrases. Join us and embark on a journey to master language skills!


(卡片2)
AI 工具入門

AI Tools Tutorial

 

本課程旨在教學如何編寫用於生成式AI 模型的生成指令(prompts)，課程中會帶領學員於Stable Diffusion、Midjourney等軟體平台中進行生成影像的應用練習， 並以ChatGPT 4.5、Gemini 3.1、Grok 4.20等作為自然語言生成與核心指令編輯的教學工具。

 

This course aims to teach participants how to design and write prompts for generative AI models. Throughout the course, learners will engage in hands-on practice in AI image generation using platforms such as Stable Diffusion and Midjourney.

In addition, tools including ChatGPT 4.5, Gemini 3.1, and Grok 4.20 will be used as core instructional resources for natural language generation and prompt engineering.

 

使用平台及軟體 Used platforms and software 

Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, 

ChatGPT 4.5, Gemini 3.1, and Grok 4.20

多益與中級/中高級全民英檢

TOEIC & GEPT (Intermediate to Upper-Intermediate) Preparation

 

搶先一步，掌握多益與GEPT全民英檢的課程！本課程深入解析考試結構，提供針對性練習，強化聽、說、讀、寫各項技能。透過實際模擬考試及題海戰術，助您信心滿滿迎戰檢定。不僅滿足考試需求，更培養實用英語應用能力。立即參與，奠定英語檢定成功基石！

 

Stay ahead and master TOEIC & the GEPT General English Proficiency Test course! This program provides in-depth analysis of the exam structure, targeted exercises, and strengthens listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills. Through practical simulated exams and strategic question-solving tactics, boost your confidence to face the test. Not only meeting exam requirements but also cultivating practical English proficiency. Join now to lay the foundation for success in the English proficiency test!
(卡片4)
直播與自媒體應用

Live Streaming & Self-Media Courses

 

這門課程致力於教導學員運用攝像鏡頭和串流軟體，實現無縫的實時直播體驗。其中將涵蓋鏡頭設置、腳本設計、串流軟體及插件應用(OBS Studio、StreamElements)、直播平台(Facebook, Youtube, Twitch)的使用技巧及虛擬形像軟體應用(Animaze、VTube Studio)。透過理論講解和實際操作演練，學員將掌握個人開台及進行直播活動的實用技能。

 

This course is designed to equip participants with the skills to utilize cameras and streaming software to deliver seamless real-time live streaming experiences. The course covers camera setup, script design, and the use of streaming software and plugins, including OBS Studio and StreamElements.

It also introduces live streaming platforms such as Facebook, YouTube, and Twitch, as well as virtual avatar applications like Animaze and VTube Studio.

Through a combination of theoretical instruction and hands-on practice, participants will develop practical skills for launching personal live streams and managing live streaming activities effectively.

 

使用平台和軟體 Used platforms and software 

OBS Studio, StreamElements, Facebook, Youtube, Twitch,

Animaze and VTube Studio
